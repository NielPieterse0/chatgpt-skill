# Evaluation and statistics

Load for model comparison, uncertainty, robustness, statistical tests, or research disposition.

Verify evaluation dataset identity, point-in-time validity, split isolation, target integrity, and metric definitions before scoring. Compare baselines and candidates under one protocol. Report primary metric with uncertainty plus relevant secondary metrics; analyze errors by meaningful segments and failure categories.

State the estimand/hypothesis before choosing a test. Check independence, pairing, clustering, time dependence, censoring, and imbalance. Prefer effect size and uncertainty first; use bootstrap/permutation/randomization methods when assumptions are weak. Account for multiple comparisons and repeated experimentation. Test sensitivity across seeds, periods, subgroups, preprocessing choices, and outlier treatments when material.

Keep final-test evaluation separate from tuning and preserve negative/failed evaluations.
